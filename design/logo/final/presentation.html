What we heard

The brief

An open-source, self-hosted, local-first notes app where everything is an object: pages, tasks, books, whiteboards. The mark should feel calm, warm and personal, read at 16 px, and stand apart from the purple gems, cubes and letters-in-a-box of its category.

vastcalmwarmyours

Success: a mark that is its own (not a library icon), works as app icon and favicon, in one colour, on the app's warm light and dark backgrounds.

Vasta — identity concepts2
Concept A

Macio

A soft tile — an object — with a V cut from its top: the room your notes live in.

  • The tile is Vasta's object; the V cut makes it ours and names it
  • One radius family on every curve: soft, calm, never sharp
  • Its own small-size cut keeps the V open at 16 px
  • Warm ink and paper from the app; Vasta blue as the accent
